## Software {#software}

I choose software with two principles:

1.  Data remains accessible and usable even if the software is no longer available.
2.  The software should seamlessly fit into my workflow.

With the principles, I prefer open source software to proprietary ones:

-   [Emacs](https://www.gnu.org/software/emacs/emacs.html) for [coding, reading and writing](https://github.com/goofansu/emacs-config/blob/f79f654402526bb06618dcf84825803aa3997cf2/init.el#L22-L25)
-   [Zed](https://zed.dev/) for [agentic coding]({{< relref "agentic-coding" >}})
-   [Ghostty](https://ghostty.org/) and [fish shell](https://fishshell.com/) for great terminal experience
